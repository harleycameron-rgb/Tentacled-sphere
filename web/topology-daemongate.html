Six faces → Cube genesis

Six origin-forming HTML interfaces each contribute a structural digest. Under domain separation, the six candidate 256-bit commitments concatenate into a 1536-bit topology commitment C_genesis, bound to the geometric origin [1024, 1024, 0].

The 1536-bit encoding is a candidate contract, not a claim of 1536-bit cryptographic strength. C_genesis is a commitment, never an encryption key. The Cube is conceptually frozen after finalisation. Current research simulations do not execute a native genesis burn.

Eight Sphere interfaces

After conceptual genesis, eight independent HTML interfaces attach to the fixed origin. Each expresses relative movement (Δx, Δy, Δz, Δθ) and reciprocal inputs/outputs under explicit port contracts. Existing browser routes retain their current names until a separately reviewed migration.
