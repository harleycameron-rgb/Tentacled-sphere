Sentinel, Cube and Six Commitments

The geometry before the proposed separation boundary.

The Sentinel anchors provenance. The Cube describes three spatial axes with opposing face pairs, represented by six named commitments: Tap, Surface, Gain, Habitat, Engine and Business.

The shared reference origin is [1024,1024,0]. No fourth spatial axis is introduced at the cube lock.

Seated tentacles

The current research interpretation keeps the six spatial tentacles seated in their cube-face relationships. A separation event need not mean that these commitments physically detach or disappear.

What is tested?

Tests can check whether all six named commitments are supplied and whether their recorded geometry is internally consistent. They do not prove that a physical cube or tentacle exists.
