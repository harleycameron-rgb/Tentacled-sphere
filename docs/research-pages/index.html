Waxtablet Research

Modular research into interaction, drift, and traceable adaptation.

Waxtablet investigates how complex geometric relationships can be described, tested and compared while preserving a traceable record of their originating conditions.

SCANDOC Zero is an experimental architecture described through Sentinel, Cube and Sphere. This site explains the proposed geometry, the separation boundary and the difference between a computational test and evidence of real-world execution.

Research principles

Preserve origin. Separate geometric commitments from event receipts. Test post-transition relationships instead of assuming continuity. Report unknowns without disguising them as successes.

Research status: conceptual architecture and draft simulation tests. Not an AGI demonstration, medical device, or verified physical mechanism.
