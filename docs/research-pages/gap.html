The 1536-bit Planck Gap

A boundary where continuity must not be assumed.

SCANDOC proposes a single 1536-bit system-state snapshot associated with the six-face separation event. The snapshot is a record of a proposed boundary, not proof that any invariant survives it.

Planck Threshold Non-Assumption: no geometric, temporal or physical invariant is presumed to survive the proposed gap merely because it held before it. Post-gap claims require separate tests.

What remains unknown?

The canonical encoding from six face commitments to the 1536-bit state is not implemented. The draft bridge currently accepts an opaque 1536-bit value; it does not establish its derivation from the six commitments.

The term “Planck gap” is a research metaphor for a computational or conceptual boundary. It is not an established physical discontinuity at Planck length or Planck time.
