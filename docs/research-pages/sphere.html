TentacledSphere and Temporal Expression

Post-gap relationships without invented geometry.

The Sphere is a proposed post-boundary geometric expression. Six spatial tentacles retain their cube commitments as provenance. Two further relationships are described as the Time Axis and the Foretentacle.

The Time Axis refers back to the Sentinel genesis; the Foretentacle expresses the present-facing boundary under the rule no forward of now. These are architectural descriptions, not independently measured temporal dimensions.

Separation versus trajectory

The current hypothesis allows trajectory to be a transition at the Planck gap rather than prolonged movement away from the Cube. This remains a design question.

Implementation limit

Generating eight tip geometries from the separation snapshot has not been implemented. Draft modules accept externally supplied expressions and cannot prove that the sphere arose from the cube.
